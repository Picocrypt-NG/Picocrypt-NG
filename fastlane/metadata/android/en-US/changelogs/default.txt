v2.19
- Added a curated Russian UI translation.
- Cancelled operations no longer enter the successful save flow, and stale temporary files are removed safely at startup.
- Compressed single-file exports now use .zip.pcv names.
- Android 7.0+ remains supported, but new APKs are 64-bit-only for ARM64 and x86-64; historical 32-bit APKs remain available with older releases.
